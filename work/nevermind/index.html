nevermind

2014

This series explores the formal intervention of iconic images with the aim of subverting their original meaning. Through a critical approach, it seeks to contradict established visual narratives in order to generate new readings — whether ironic, dramatic, or disruptive. The intention is not only to transform the image, but also to reactivate its symbolic power, inviting the viewer to reestablish its original impact.
