pausa

2007

← trompe l´oeilworkaquí es todas partes →

Images where nothing specific is taking place, just a pause in the flow of infinite everyday images. The scenery in this series is no more than accidental. Thanks to the figure, space loses its ideal quality and converts it into movement alone. The intention is to capture the fleeting moment, to distill what we only see out of the corner of our eye. In principle, the idea of “pause” is to find that space where what apparently is nothing at all, in its immediate and evanescent nature, somehow surprises us with the fullness of everything.

— Ana Elena Mallet
