bob ross disturbed

2017

In this series I juxtapose two visual languages: the digital world, represented by pixels, and traditional oil painting, in genres such as landscape and still life. This series explores the tension between the analog and the digital, confronting their aesthetics, methods, and meanings. The work seeks to generate a visual dialogue that brings both universes together, inviting the viewer to reflect on their coincidences and contradictions, their benefits and harms, their consonances and dissonances.
